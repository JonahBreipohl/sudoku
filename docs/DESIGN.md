# Design: Hanami

The look of 2010s TV anime applied to a calm puzzle app: clean ink linework, flat saturated color
with one hard shadow, screentone dots, speed lines and a flame-like aura for the big moment. No
characters, names, emblems or series symbols, and no decorative Japanese text. Play stays
minimal; the sky and blossoms frame the board and only move in answer to the player.

Where things live: tokens in `src/ui/styles.css` (light, and dark twice: the media query and the
`data-theme` attribute), Display in `src/ui/display/display.css`, icons in `src/ui/icons.tsx`
(generated by `scripts/gen-brush-icons.mjs`), app icons in `public/icons/` (generated by
`scripts/gen-app-icons.mjs`), fonts in `public/fonts/`.

## Type

Both faces are self-hosted latin subsets (woff2, `font-display: swap`) with their OFL licenses
beside them in `public/fonts/`. The three weights of the UI face and the title face are
precached by the service worker like every other build file.

| Role | Face | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Dela Gothic One 400 | The "Sudoku" wordmark, dialog titles, the solved moment. Nowhere else. `font-synthesis: none` so it never fakes a bold. |
| UI and digits | M PLUS Rounded 1c 400, 700, 800 | Everything else. Givens 800, entries 400 slanted, notes 700 and small, buttons 700, emphasis 800. |

The UI face gives every digit one width at each weight (checked with fontTools: 620, 640 and 650
units at 400, 700 and 800), so the Display clock cannot jitter when the seconds change. It has no
`tnum` feature because it does not need one; the clock sets `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`
anyway and an e2e check measures the width of different times. The digit and title faces are
preloaded in `index.html`.

## Components and states

Shared look for every button (`.ctl`, the way home, pad keys): 2px ink outline, 6px radius, a
`3px 3px 0` cel shadow. Press moves the button `translate(2px, 2px)` while the shadow shrinks to
`1px`, so it clicks. Hover (fine pointers only) brightens the fill; focus-visible is a 3px ring
in the accent, offset 4px so it clears the shadow. Disabled buttons turn flat and dashed.

Display: the clock, date, stats and Spotify card share one paper panel (the card is flat inside
it, under a dashed rule); the board is a second panel with the same frame and shadow. The clock is
800 weight with a blossom-shade cel offset. Controls and Exit are normal buttons; a pressed
toggle is filled and 3px heavy as well as labeled "on". Night dimming (22:00 to 07:00) filters
the whole body, panels included.

## Icons

One family in `src/ui/icons.tsx`: filled paths with tapered stroke ends on a 24px grid,
`currentColor`, `aria-hidden`, sized in `em`. Each is the outline of a variable-width brush
stroke, generated from centerlines by `scripts/gen-brush-icons.mjs`.

undo, check, x, sun, moon, erase, pause, play, prev, next, star, hourglass, dot, arrowLeft,
pencil, note, alert, diamond, question, sparkle, expand, shrink, eye, sliders.

Replaced glyphs: undo, check and cross (Controls, status, dialogs, pad, cell badges), sun and
moon (theme toggle), erase (pad), pause (overlay and Spotify), play, previous and next (Spotify),
star (daily), hourglass (generating), dot (assistance), arrow (way home, Exit Display), pencil
(notes hint), note (Spotify), and the Display controls (expand, shrink, eye, sliders).

## App icons

`public/icons/icon.svg`: a five-petal blossom, two-tone with a hard shadow and ink outlines,
whose heart is a tiny 3 by 3 grid with three digits marked, on a flat sky. Rasterized with
Playwright: `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png` (full bleed, blossom inside
the 80% safe circle; source `icon-maskable.svg`), `apple-touch-icon.png` (180, full bleed) and
`favicon-32.png`. The manifest background and theme color are the day sky `#9bd6f6`;
`index.html` carries one `theme-color` per scheme (`#9bd6f6`, `#1b1a3f`).

## Motion

- Press: 120ms translate and shadow. Hover and state colors: 120ms. Dialog: 160ms fade and scale.
- Existing entry animations (digit pop, note fade, shake, sweep, ripple) are unchanged.
- Solved aura (`Aura.tsx`): a fixed layer behind the app, centered on the board, three layers
  (a radial halo and two interleaved tongue rings from conic gradients) that scale, turn and fade
  for about 2s with transform and opacity only, then remove themselves. Under reduced motion the
  same layers fade in and out in place with no movement. It overrides the global reduced-motion
  clamp on purpose, since the glow is the feedback.
- Speed lines (`SpeedLines.tsx`): static SVG wedges behind the solved title, masked clear in the
  middle. No motion at all.
- Nothing loops. Display stays idle between clock ticks.

## For the 3D scene

The app root is `position: relative; z-index: 1` and has no background, so a canvas or sky layer
behind it shows through everywhere except the opaque panels. The aura layer sits at `z-index: 0`
in `body`, above anything with a lower z-index. `--bg` is the flat sky color to paint under it.
`.display` still paints `--bg`; the scene should replace that background when it mounts.

## Checked against the brief

Things that read generic on the first pass and what changed:

- Plain colored "Sudoku" heading: it sat directly on the scene and could not guarantee contrast.
  It is now a blossom title plate in the display face, an ink-outlined sticker with a cel shadow.
- Controls on the sky: the brief wants controls on opaque surfaces. The toolbar, status, hint and
  pad now share one paper panel, and the "Generating" label and the way-home link are paper pills.
- One shadow style for everything: kept, but panels (`6px`), buttons (`3px`) and the panel under
  the board (`4px`) step down so the board stays the hero.
- Left accent stripe on the hint panel (a known tell): replaced by a full ink outline, a sparkle
  icon and a cel shadow.
- Tracked all-caps eyebrow ("HINT 2 OF 3"): now sentence case in the accent weight.
- Conflict reading as more pink: the wash is peach, the digit vermilion, and the wavy underline is
  kept as the real cue.
- Dark mode outlines: ink-on-dark vanished, so outlines are pale rim light and only the shadow is
  near-black.
- Decorative glow halo for the aura: it is built from tongues and a halo that answer the solve,
  fire once and go away, not an ambient radial glow.
- Nested cards on Display: the Spotify card lost its own border and background inside the clock
  panel.
- The display face is held to the wordmark, dialog titles and the solved title. The pause title,
  stats and clock use the UI face.
- Hover: only a brighter fill on fine pointers; no lift on cards.
- Entry digits stay slanted: a synthesized oblique of a rounded gothic reads like manga lettering
  and keeps the existing non-color cue.
